The Lifetime List · tool-brands

  • Channellock 420 tongue-and-groove pliers — Meadville, Pennsylvania. The DeArment family, fifth generation, forging its own carbon steel. Limited lifetime warranty. Under $20. See it on Amazon
  • Klein Tools lineman's pliers — The company Mathias Klein started in Chicago in 1857, still run by his family. Seven US factories. Limited lifetime warranty on most hand tools. See it on Amazon
  • Estwing E3-16C 16 oz hammer — Rockford, Illinois, since 1923. Head and handle forged from one piece of steel. Warranted against failure in normal use (not a lifetime warranty). $20-30 depending on the retailer. See it on Amazon
  • Wright Tool combination wrenches — Barberton, Ohio, since 1927. Owned since 2007 by its own managers, not a conglomerate or a fund. Still drop-forged in Barberton. See it on Amazon
