The Lifetime List · washers

  • Speed Queen TC5 (top load) — Built in Ripon, Wisconsin by Alliance Laundry Systems. Metal transmission, parts available for decades. About $1,500 on the shelf today. See it on Amazon
  • Keep your Kenmore 110-series — It is a Whirlpool direct-drive. The part that fails most often, the motor coupling (Whirlpool 285753A), costs under $20. Repair it, do not replace it. See it on Amazon code 110
  • Refrigerators: buy on the compressor — Conventional compressor, no linear, no Wi-Fi, no screen. Check the first three digits of the model number before the door.
