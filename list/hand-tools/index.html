The Lifetime List · hand-tools

  • Channellock 420 tongue-and-groove pliers — Forged in Meadville, Pennsylvania, by the same family since 1886. About $30 on the shelf today; the pair mechanics hand down. See it on Amazon
  • Wright Tool combination wrench set — Forged and finished in Barberton, Ohio. Costs about three times a Craftsman set at Lowe's and is what the old -V- Craftsman was trying to be. See it on Amazon
  • Keep your old Craftsman (-V- and WF marks) — American-forged, lifetime warranty still honored at Lowe's. Fill the gaps from the secondhand market instead of buying the new import. See it on Amazon code -V- / WF
