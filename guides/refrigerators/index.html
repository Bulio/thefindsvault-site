Seven names in one aisle, three owners and one builder. None of this is printed on the door, and all of it is in the sources below.

The one part you can actually buy

The compressor is not a shelf item and we would not pretend otherwise. The water filter is, and here the badge on the door finally means something: the filter is made by the company that built the machine.

EDR1RXD1

everydrop by Whirlpool Ice & Water Filter 1 (EDR1RXD1)

Whirlpool's own filter line, which covers the KitchenAid, Maytag, Amana and JennAir badges as well. Filter 1 is the purple one; the number printed on the filter you are pulling out, or the manual, says which of the line fits. Aftermarket copies of the same number exist and cost less — that is a decision, not an accident.
