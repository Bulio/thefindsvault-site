Keep the one you have: the parts that actually fail

A Whirlpool direct-drive washer — which is what most Kenmore 110-series and most post-2006 Maytag top-loaders are underneath — fails in three predictable places. All three are parts you can buy for the price of a service call.

  • Motor coupling, 285753A (metal core) — The part that fails first and most often. Plastic ends wear, the motor spins, the tub does not. See it on Amazon →
  • Motor coupling, 285753A — BlueStars metal core — Same part, the version most repair shops keep on the van. Check the current listing →
  • Clutch assembly, 285785 (Whirlpool OEM) — Genuine Whirlpool. If the tub spins slowly or squeals on spin-up, this is it. Look it up on Amazon →
  • The three-part kit: clutch, coupling and agitator dogs — Everything above in one box. Cheaper than buying them one failure at a time. See what it costs today →
  • Agitator dogs, 80040 (4-pack) — When the agitator turns one way and the clothes do not move, it is these. Find it on Amazon →
  • Lid switch, 3949238 — Washer fills but will not spin, and there is no error code: check this first. See it on Amazon →
