Washer parts: which makers still sell the bearing, and the three direct-drive parts that fail

Three washers where the maker sells only the big part

When the drum bearing wears out, these makers' parts catalogues offer the tub, not the bearing. For two of them, other companies sell bearing kits. Those kits are not made by the washer's maker, and fitting one means taking the machine apart and pressing the old bearings out of the tub: a job for a repair shop or a very patient owner.

  • Whirlpool Duet family: rear outer tub W10772617 (genuine Whirlpool) — Front-loaders sold as Whirlpool, Maytag and Kenmore. The genuine part for a worn bearing is the rear outer tub that holds it. Prices we found on 21 September 2026: $326.99 on sale at ApplianceParts4All, $371.95 at Parts Town, $389.68 at The Home Depot's repair parts store, $508.29 at AppliancePartsPros. code W10772617
  • Bearing and seal kit for the Duet family (not made by Whirlpool) — Sold by other companies as a replacement for Whirlpool and Maytag front-loaders, listed under W10772619, W10290562 and W10283358. It is not a Whirlpool part: check it against your model number before ordering. Check the current listing →
  • Frigidaire and Electrolux: rear outer tub 131525500 (genuine) — Front-loaders sold as Frigidaire, Electrolux and some Kenmore models. The listing says the bearing and shaft seal come with the tub and are not sold separately. $367.99 on 21 September 2026, down from $427.99, at ApplianceParts4All. code 131525500
  • Bearing and seal kit made for tub 131525500 (not made by Electrolux) — A front bearing, a rear bearing and a front seal, with Nachi bearings made in Japan. $79.95 at Front Load Bearings when we checked. The whole washer comes apart to fit it.
  • LG top-loaders without an agitator: tub assembly drum AJQ74873903 (genuine LG) — For models such as the WT7000CW and WT8200CW. $656.95 on LG's own parts store, the most expensive single part in the episode. We did not find a bearing sold on its own for these two models. code AJQ74873903

Three washers where the maker still sells the bearing

Here the company that built the washer sells the bearing on its own. Read the model number on the sticker before you order: the same brand can sit on both sides of this page.

  • LG front-loaders: rear tub bearing MAP61913707 (genuine LG) — Fits front-loaders such as the WM3400CW and WM3600HWA. $42.95 on LG's parts store, and the same $42.95 at Parts Town. Same brand as the $656.95 drum above: the model number decides which one you own. code MAP61913707
  • Samsung: tub bearing 6601-002637 (genuine Samsung) — Sold on its own for $83.95 on Samsung's parts site. For some models Samsung also sells the complete front outer tub, DC97-15596A, at $170.99 on iFixit. Both come from the maker; check which one your machine needs. code 6601-002637
  • GE: tub bearing WH2X1198 (genuine GE Appliances) — Sold as a standalone genuine part for some GE washers. Match it to your model number before you order. Look it up on Amazon → code WH2X1198

Keep the one you have: the parts that actually fail

The Whirlpool direct-drive washer — the platform behind a great many Kenmore 110-series machines — fails in three predictable places. All three are parts you can buy for the price of a service call.

  • Motor coupling, 285753A (metal core) — The part that fails first and most often. Plastic ends wear, the motor spins, the tub does not. See it on Amazon →
  • Motor coupling, 285753A — BlueStars metal core — Same part, the version most repair shops keep on the van. Check the current listing →
  • Clutch assembly, 285785 (Whirlpool OEM) — Genuine Whirlpool. If the tub spins slowly or squeals on spin-up, this is it. Look it up on Amazon →
  • The three-part kit: clutch, coupling and agitator dogs — Everything above in one box. Cheaper than buying them one failure at a time. See what it costs today →
  • Agitator dogs, 80040 (4-pack) — When the agitator turns one way and the clothes do not move, it is these. Find it on Amazon →
  • Lid switch, 3949238 — Washer fills but will not spin, and there is no error code: check this first. See it on Amazon →

If you are buying new

Full-size washers are not sold through Amazon, so there is no honest link to put here. These are the machines the episode names — buy them from a local dealer, and read the model number before you read the badge.

  • Speed Queen TC5 (top load) — Built in Ripon, Wisconsin, by Alliance Laundry Systems. Metal transmission, parts available for decades. Sold through appliance dealers, not Amazon.
  • Keep your Kenmore 110-series — A 110 prefix means Whirlpool built it. If yours is the older direct-drive machine, the three parts above cover almost everything that goes wrong with one — check the model number against a parts catalogue first.
  • Refrigerators: buy on the compressor — Conventional compressor, no linear, no Wi-Fi, no screen. Check the first three digits of the model number before the badge.
