Three washers where the maker sells only the big part

When the drum bearing wears out, these makers' parts catalogues offer the tub, not the bearing. For two of them, other companies sell bearing kits. Those kits are not made by the washer's maker, and fitting one means taking the machine apart and pressing the old bearings out of the tub: a job for a repair shop or a very patient owner.

W10772617

Whirlpool Duet family: rear outer tub W10772617 (genuine Whirlpool)

Front-loaders sold as Whirlpool, Maytag and Kenmore. The genuine part for a worn bearing is the rear outer tub that holds it. Prices we found on 21 September 2026: $326.99 on sale at ApplianceParts4All, $371.95 at Parts Town, $389.68 at The Home Depot's repair parts store, $508.29 at AppliancePartsPros.
