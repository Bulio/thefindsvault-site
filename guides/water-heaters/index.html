The rod that decides the tank

The anode corrodes instead of your tank. When it is gone, the tank starts to rust — that is the manufacturer's own wording, not ours. Match the length to your tank and the thread to your fitting before ordering: 3/4 inch NPT is the common one, and a flexible rod is what you need when there is no ceiling clearance above the heater.

Before you replace the whole thing

A rod plus an hour of labour against a new heater installed. The arithmetic is usually not close.
