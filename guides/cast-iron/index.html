Read the bottom of the pan

What you seeWhat it means
A raised ring on the basea heat ring — common on pans made for wood and coal ranges, and on early electric
A mirror-smooth cooking surfacethe pan was machined or polished after casting: a step dropped by mass producers mid-century
A sandy, pebbled surfaceraw sand-cast finish, seasoned over: normal on a modern mass-market pan
No name at allunmarked pans were made by many foundries, including ones that no longer exist in any form
