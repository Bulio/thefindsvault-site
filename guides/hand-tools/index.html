American-made hand tools still worth buying, brand by brand

As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases. The product links on this page are affiliate links.

Most of the names on a hardware store wall no longer belong to the company that forged the tool. Four do, and they still run their own plants in the United States: Channellock in Meadville, Klein in Chicago, Estwing in Rockford, Wright Tool in Barberton. This page lists what each of them still makes, what it costs, and which of the old tools already in your box are worth keeping instead of replacing.
